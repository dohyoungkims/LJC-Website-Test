The project

A full-floor workplace amenity suite shaped by the warmth and ease of hospitality.

LJC reimagined the amenity floor at 191 N Wacker as a connected setting for work, gathering, wellness, and quiet retreat. Conference rooms, fitness facilities, and social spaces sit within one continuous tenant experience.

Hearth and bar lounges create places to meet or pause, with the Chicago River and skyline as a backdrop. Wood and stone bring warmth to a restrained material palette.

As design architect and architect of record, LJC carried the work from programming and design through documentation, construction administration, and furniture coordination.
